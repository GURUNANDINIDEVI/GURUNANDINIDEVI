## 🧠 About Me

🎓 **ECE Student | Aspiring VLSI Design Verification Engineer**

I specialize in **functional verification of digital designs**, creating **SystemVerilog testbenches**, validating **RTL logic**, and ensuring **protocol correctness** through simulation and waveform debugging.

I enjoy breaking designs in simulation so that **real silicon never fails**.
